Why Shopify CSV imports fail

Shopify's product importer is strict, and its error messages often don't say which row is wrong. These are the problems this checker looks for:

File problems

  • Wrong encoding. Excel's "CSV" (not "CSV UTF-8") saves accents and symbols in Windows-1252; they import as garbled text like Ã© or â€™.
  • Semicolon or tab separators from European Excel or supplier systems.
  • Unclosed quotes that swallow the rest of the file into one cell, and rows with more cells than the header (data shifted into the wrong columns).
  • Duplicate or missing columns — a "Title" column is always required, and a handle column is required for variants.

Product and variant problems

  • Invalid handles with spaces, capitals or symbols (only letters, numbers and dashes are allowed).
  • Two different products sharing one handle — Shopify merges them into one product.
  • Rows of one product scattered through the file instead of next to each other.
  • Duplicate variants (same Size/Color twice) and missing option values on variant rows.
  • SKUs and barcodes destroyed by Excel, e.g. 8.90123E+11 — the original digits are gone.

Value problems

  • Prices with $, €, thousands separators or decimal commas (19,99); empty prices that import as 0.00; compare-at price not above the price.
  • Weights like 0.5 kg in a column that expects whole grams.
  • Yes/No instead of true/false; invalid Status (must be active, draft or archived).
  • Image URLs without https://, with spaces, or pointing at _small/_thumb low-resolution files.
  • SEO fields longer than Shopify shows (title 70, description 320 characters) and more than 250 tags.
