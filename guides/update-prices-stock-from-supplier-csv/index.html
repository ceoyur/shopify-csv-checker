Update Shopify prices and inventory from a supplier CSV safely

How to update Shopify prices and stock from a supplier price list (CSV)

What it means

Suppliers usually send a spreadsheet keyed by SKU or barcode, while Shopify's product CSV is keyed by handle and option values. Updating prices and stock means matching the two, applying your markup, and building two different files: a product CSV for prices and an inventory CSV for quantities.

Common causes

  • Blank cells in an overwrite import erase existing values — a price file must not contain empty columns you didn't mean to change.
  • Option values without the option names can trigger “Option value provided for unknown options”.
  • Missing supplier rows or text like “in stock” must not become 0, or you'll mark items sold out.
  • Shopify's inventory import sets quantities rather than adding to them; it compares “On hand (current)” with live stock to catch sales made after your export.
  • Excel can turn supplier SKUs and barcodes into scientific notation (4.00638E+12), breaking the match.

How to fix it

  1. Export your products (Products → Export) and inventory (Products → Inventory → Export) first and keep them as a backup.
  2. Match supplier rows to variants by SKU or barcode; skip SKUs that appear on more than one variant.
  3. Build the price file with handle, title, option names and values, and only the price/compare-at/cost columns — every included cell filled.
  4. Build the stock file from the inventory export for one location, keeping “On hand (current)” and filling “On hand (new)” only where the number changes.
  5. Review big price moves and prices below cost, test with 2–3 products, then import: Products → Import (tick overwrite) and Products → Inventory → Import.
