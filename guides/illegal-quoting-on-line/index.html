Fix “Illegal quoting on line” and “Missing or stray quote” in a Shopify CSV

Illegal quoting on line / Missing or stray quote on line

What it means

CSV files use straight double quotes (") to wrap cells that contain commas or line breaks. When a quote is opened and never closed, or appears in the wrong place, the parser can't tell where cells end. Shopify's help also links this error to files that are not UTF-8 encoded.

Common causes

  • Curly “smart” quotes inserted by Excel or a word processor.
  • Inch and foot marks in sizes, like 12" or 6' x 9', that weren't escaped.
  • A quote opened in a description and never closed — everything after it is read as one giant cell.
  • The file was saved in a non-UTF-8 encoding.

How to fix it

  1. Open the CSV in a plain text editor and go to the line number in the error.
  2. Replace curly quotes with straight ones, or remove them.
  3. Write sizes as 12 in / 6 ft x 9 ft, or make sure the cell is wrapped in quotes with inner quotes doubled ("12""").
  4. Save as CSV UTF-8, or export from Google Sheets.
