Shopify reorder points without Stocky: what to reorder, how many and when

How to calculate reorder points and quantities in Shopify (after Stocky)

What it means

Stocky, Shopify's free purchasing app, was retired on 31 August 2026. Shopify's native purchase orders let you write an order, but they don't tell you which products to reorder or how many. You can calculate it from three standard exports: orders, inventory and products.

Common causes

  • Sales per day = units sold in the last 30/60/90 days ÷ days in that window. Ignore cancelled and fully refunded orders.
  • Days of cover = (available + incoming) ÷ sales per day. Counting incoming stops you ordering the same thing twice.
  • Reorder now when days of cover < supplier lead time + safety days.
  • Order quantity = sales per day × (lead time + days until your next order + safety days) − available − incoming, rounded up to the supplier's pack size.
  • Decide per supplier: when one product from a supplier must be ordered, add that supplier's other products that would run low before your next order — one shipment instead of two.

How to fix it

  1. Export orders for the last 60–90 days: Orders → Export → CSV.
  2. Export inventory: Products → Inventory → Export (it has Available and Incoming per location).
  3. Export products for vendor and cost per item: Products → Export.
  4. Join them by SKU, calculate the numbers above per variant, then group by vendor.
  5. Watch out: the exports don't include stock history, so days a product was sold out make its sales rate look lower. Add safety days for best-sellers that were out of stock.
