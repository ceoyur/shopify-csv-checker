Fix “Inventory policy is not included in the list” (Shopify CSV)

Inventory policy is not included in the list

What it means

The out-of-stock policy column (Continue selling when out of stock in the current template, Variant Inventory Policy in the older one) only accepts deny or continue.

Common causes

  • Values such as Yes, No, TRUE, allow or Deny with extra text.
  • Columns shifted by one because of a stray quote or comma, so another column's value landed here.

How to fix it

  1. Use deny (stop selling at 0) or continue (allow overselling).
  2. If many values look wrong at once, check the row for shifted cells first.
  3. Related: Inventory quantity is not a number — use whole numbers only (5, not 5 pcs or 5.5); and Inventory quantity can't be blank — if you don't track inventory, leave the inventory tracker column empty.
