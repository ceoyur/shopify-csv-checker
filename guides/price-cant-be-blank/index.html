Validation failed: price can't be blank

What it means

A variant row has no usable price. Shopify expects a plain number with a dot as the decimal separator and no currency symbol.

Common causes

  • The price cell is empty on a variant row.
  • Prices with currency symbols or codes: $19.99, 19.99 USD.
  • Decimal commas or thousands separators: 19,99, 1.234,50.
  • Compare-at price equal to or lower than the price — it imports, but no sale price is shown.

How to fix it

  1. Fill a price on every variant row.
  2. Format as 19.99: digits, one dot, no symbol, no thousands separator.
  3. Make the compare-at price higher than the price, or leave it empty.
